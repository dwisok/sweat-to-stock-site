The idea

The ground first.
Then the world.

We don't sell a dream. We count strides. Your phone records the proof, the protocol verifies it, and the reward is a formula you can check. The rest is you.

  • Stock tokens on Robinhood Chain: AAPLx, NVDAx, TSLAx and 90 more, tradable 24/7.
  • In your own wallet. Swap them, hold them, use them as collateral.
  • Only verified kilometres pay. The reward is km × 100 × quality, on-chain.

The app

01

Connect, then run

Your Robinhood Wallet is the account. No sign-up, no broker form. Your ROAD balance and what's claimable sit on the home screen.

02

Proof of run

The app records the GPS track, cadence and elevation at 1 Hz. This is the evidence, not the distance on its own.

03

Verified, then paid in ROAD

Every kilometre passes four checks. The reward is a formula, not a promise: km × 100 × quality. A clean 8.4 km run is 840 ROAD.

04

Claim into stock tokens

Pick a stock, press Claim, confirm in the wallet. ROAD is swapped on-chain into a Stock Token on Robinhood Chain. One transaction, 100 ms blocks, a hash you can open.

05

Your portfolio, on-chain

Stock Tokens are plain ERC-20s in your wallet: hold them, swap them on Uniswap, use them as collateral. Every claim is on the proof ledger.

Questions

Straight answers.

What do I actually receive?

Stock Tokens on Robinhood Chain: ERC-20 tokens that track a listed stock and trade 24/7. They live in your own wallet, not in our app. To be clear, they give economic exposure to the underlying stock, not legal ownership of the shares.

How do you know I really ran?

The app records a proof of run: GPS track at 1 Hz, cadence, elevation, and how it compares to your history. Four checks produce a quality score between 0 and 1, and the reward is km × 100 × quality. A run that fails the checks scores 0 and earns nothing.

Does it cost anything?

No subscription. A claim is one on-chain swap, and the protocol keeps a small fee on it. If you don't run, we don't earn. Gas on Robinhood Chain is a fraction of a cent.
